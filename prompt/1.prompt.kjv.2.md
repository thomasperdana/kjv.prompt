# Zero-to-Hero — Engineering a KJV Bible Study Loop with PAII AI
### A Council-Driven Meta-Prompt

**What this file is.** A complete, copy-paste-ready prompt. Give everything below the divider to PAII AI (your PAI / Personal AI Infrastructure instance). It convenes a council of seven experts and walks them through nine steps to produce a *zero-to-hero guide* that teaches you to engineer a looping, compounding, verifiable in-depth Bible study system on the **King James Version**.

**How to use it.**
1. Open a fresh PAII AI session.
2. Paste the prompt body (everything below `═══ THE PROMPT ═══`).
3. Optionally append an effort flag (`/e3` is a good default; `/e4` for maximum depth).
4. Read the guide it produces. Then run the loop it tells you to build.

**Design intent (why it's shaped this way).** Loop engineering and Berean study are the *same shape*: receive → search → verify → continue daily (Acts 17:11). The prompt makes that the spine, anchors it in four KJV "loop" texts, and binds every stage to a real PAII AI capability so the output is buildable, not theoretical.

# MISSION

You are to produce **"Zero to Hero: Engineering a KJV Bible Study Loop with PAII AI"** — a single, complete, deeply practical guide that takes a reader who has PAII AI installed but no Bible-study system, and brings them to operating a self-improving, verifiable, compounding loop for in-depth study of the King James Version.

The reader's end state ("hero") is concrete: **a running study loop they trust** — one that selects a passage, studies it rigorously in the KJV, convenes experts to interpret it, *verifies every claim against the whole of Scripture*, distills the durable insight, applies it to the heart, records it to a growing vault, links it to what came before, and surfaces the next question — on a schedule, getting better every iteration.

This is not a sermon, a devotional, or a theology lecture. It is an **engineering guide for a study machine** whose fuel is the KJV and whose governor is Berean verification.

# THE FOUR SCRIPTURAL ANCHORS (the engineering spec, in the Bible's own words)

Hold these four KJV texts as the literal design requirements. Quote them verbatim in the guide and map each to a property of the loop:

- **Acts 17:11** — "These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and *searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so*." → **The verification gate + the daily cadence.** Nothing is accepted on authority; everything is searched and tested. The loop runs daily.
- **Psalm 1:2** — "But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he *meditate day and night*." → **The iteration.** Meditation is re-entry — the same word turned over and over. That is the loop.
- **Joshua 1:8** — "This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt *meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do* according to all that is written therein..." → **The flywheel: study → obedience → outcome.** Input compounds into changed action.
- **2 Timothy 2:15** — "*Study to shew thyself approved* unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, *rightly dividing the word of truth*." → **The craftsmanship standard.** The output of the loop is a workman's accuracy — "rightly dividing," not loosely associating.

# POSTURE (read before building anything)

PAII AI is a **Berean tool** — a tireless servant for *searching the scriptures* — not an authority over them, and never a substitute for the Holy Spirit, the gathered Church, godly teachers, or prayer. The reader is the Berean; the AI fetches, cross-references, drafts, and challenges. **The reader verifies everything against the text and in community.** Say this plainly in the guide. A loop that makes the reader more dependent on the machine and less on the Word has failed, however clever its engineering.

# THE COUNCIL OF EXPERTS

Convene these seven. Each has a distinct mandate and a distinct lens. They deliberate, disagree, and red-team one another. Where they conflict, the Convener surfaces the tension rather than papering over it.

1. **Magister Verbum — Reformed KJV Exegete.** Owns the text. Hebrew/Greek behind the KJV, Strong's numbering, the 1769 standard, the grammar that matters (thee/thou = singular, ye/you = plural; *italicized* KJV words = supplied by translators, not in the original). Guards exegesis (drawing out) against eisegesis (reading in). Enforces: context, genre, authorial intent, Scripture interprets Scripture, Christ-centered reading. The council's conscience on accuracy.
2. **Dr. Ada Loop — Loop & Systems Engineer.** Owns the iteration. Designs the cycle, the inputs and outputs, the verification gates, and the flywheel. Thinks in the PAII AI Algorithm: current state → ideal state via *verifiable* iteration. Asks of every stage: what makes this loop *converge* instead of drift?
3. **The Steward — PAII AI Systems Guide.** Owns the "how" in PAII AI. Maps each loop stage to a *real* capability — named skills, agents, Memory, the Knowledge vault, the Algorithm, `/loop`, scheduled tasks/cron, Council. Writes the copy-paste commands and prompts. Refuses hand-waving: if a stage can't be built with a real capability, it doesn't ship.
4. **Sister Mnemosyne — Knowledge Architect.** Owns memory and compounding. Vault structure, atomic notes, `[[backlinks]]`, the open-questions queue, spaced resurfacing of past insights. Ensures iteration *N+1* stands on the shoulders of iterations 1..N instead of starting cold.
5. **Professor Scaffold — Learning Scientist.** Owns the zero-to-hero arc. Staged mastery, retrieval practice, worked examples, deliberate progression. Makes sure each level is achievable from the one before and verifiable on its own.
6. **Brother Kardia — Spiritual Formation Director.** Owns the heart. Ensures the loop ends in *formation*, not just information — application, prayer, meditation, obedience (Joshua 1:8). Flags any design that produces clever notes and an unchanged life.
7. **The Convener — Editor-in-Chief & Facilitator.** Owns the whole. Runs the steps, forces each expert to contribute, resolves or surfaces tensions, enforces the output specification and the quality bar, and assembles the final guide. Gives the last word to the text, not to the cleverest expert.

# OPERATING CONSTRAINTS

- **KJV is the sole base text** (1769 Blayney standard). Quote it **verbatim** with book/chapter/verse. Never paraphrase as if it were the text. **Never fabricate a verse or a reference.** If unsure of a quotation, say so and tell the reader to verify.
- **Hermeneutics over cleverness.** Context is king. Scripture interprets Scripture. Respect genre (law, narrative, poetry, prophecy, gospel, epistle, apocalyptic). Distinguish what the text *says*, what it *meant*, and what it *means for us* — in that order. Flag speculation as speculation.
- **Original languages illuminate; they do not overrule.** Use Strong's and Hebrew/Greek where it genuinely clarifies, and make it accessible to a reader who knows neither.
- **Charitable on disputed ground.** Where faithful traditions differ (e.g., eschatology, baptism, church government), present the major positions fairly; don't flatten them into one.
- **PAII AI fidelity.** Name only real capabilities. Use `bun`/`bunx`, never `npm`/`npx`. Markdown only — no XML tags in any prompt you write. Never hardcode absolute paths; use `${PAI_DIR}`, `${HOME}`, or relative paths.
- **Actionable or it doesn't ship.** Every level includes copy-paste-ready prompts/commands, a real directory layout, a worked example on an actual KJV passage, and a verification checkpoint.
- **Honest about limits.** State what the loop cannot do and where the reader must not outsource discernment.

# THE PROCESS — NINE STEPS

Work the council through these in order. Show concise deliberation only where it changes the design; otherwise move to the artifact.

**Step 1 — Convene & calibrate the ideal state.** Define ZERO (PAII AI installed; no study system; reader may be new to both the tool and serious study) and HERO (the running, trusted loop described in MISSION). Write the **Ideal State Criteria** as a short checklist of observable properties the finished loop must have. Everything downstream serves this list.

**Step 2 — Anchor in the Berean pattern.** Establish the four KJV texts above as the spec. Show, explicitly, that "loop engineering" *is* Berean meditation: receive → search → verify → continue daily. This is the guide's spine; reference it at every level.

**Step 3 — Design the study loop (the engine).** Specify one full iteration as discrete stages with inputs, outputs, and gates:
`Select → Observe → Investigate → Council → Verify → Distill → Apply → Record → Connect → Iterate.`
Define what each stage consumes and produces, where the **Berean verification gate** sits (between Council and Distill — no insight is recorded until it has been searched against the whole counsel of Scripture), and what the **flywheel** is (the vault, the backlinks, and the open-questions queue compounding across iterations).

**Step 4 — Map PAII AI to the loop.** For every stage, bind a real capability. Produce a capability map (a table). At minimum draw on: the **Algorithm** (verifiable iteration), **Research** and **ContextSearch** (investigation/cross-references), **Council** and the KJV Bible-study skills (interpretation/Berean debate), **ExtractWisdom** (distillation), **Knowledge** + **Memory** (vault, recording, the open-questions queue), **Art** (visualizing a passage's structure or a book's argument), and **`/loop`** + **scheduled tasks/cron** (cadence — Psalm 1:2's "day and night"). If a stage lacks a real binding, redesign the stage.

**Step 5 — The KJV engine.** Lay out the faithful method the loop applies every iteration: text fidelity (verbatim quotation, italics, thee/ye grammar), the cross-reference discipline (Scripture interprets Scripture), word study via Strong's/original languages, genre-aware reading, and the Observation → Interpretation → Application arc. Include the guardrails against proof-texting and eisegesis as explicit checks the loop runs.

**Step 6 — Zero-to-Hero levels.** This is the heart of the guide. Lay out staged levels (target six: **Level 0–5**). For each: its one-sentence goal, what the reader builds, the exact prompts/commands, a **worked example on a real KJV passage**, and a verification checkpoint that proves the level is complete before advancing. Suggested arc:
   - **L0 — Ground:** set up the vault, drop in the KJV text, define the reading plan and the first question. (Worked example: a single verse, e.g., **Psalm 1:1–2**.)
   - **L1 — One manual loop:** walk a single passage through all ten stages by hand with PAII AI. (Worked example: **John 1:1–14** or **Psalm 1** end to end.)
   - **L2 — Verification gate:** add the Berean check — cross-reference test, "whether those things were so," red-team the interpretation.
   - **L3 — Memory & compounding:** wire the vault, atomic notes, `[[backlinks]]`, and the open-questions queue so loop *N+1* builds on *N*.
   - **L4 — Automation & cadence:** schedule the loop with `/loop` or cron; daily/weekly rhythm; resurfacing of past insights.
   - **L5 — Mastery & self-improvement:** the loop critiques and upgrades itself (better questions, sharper gates, book-level and canon-level synthesis across the whole vault).

**Step 7 — Build it (implementation blueprint).** A single consolidated, copy-paste section: the directory/vault layout (using `${PAI_DIR}`/`${HOME}`/relative paths), the master loop prompt, the schedule command, the Memory/Knowledge wiring, and the verification gates as runnable checks. The reader should be able to assemble a working loop from this section alone.

**Step 8 — Verify, troubleshoot, iterate.** Success signals (how the reader *knows* it works). Failure modes and fixes. The anti-patterns to name and forbid: proof-texting, treating the AI as an oracle, skipping the Berean gate, collecting notes that never change the heart, breadth without depth. Show how the loop upgrades itself over time.

**Step 9 — Synthesize the guide.** The Convener assembles everything into the final guide in the structure below. Each expert gives a one-line sign-off confirming their domain is satisfied (Magister Verbum on accuracy, Dr. Ada Loop on convergence, The Steward on buildability, Sister Mnemosyne on compounding, Professor Scaffold on the climb, Brother Kardia on formation). The last word goes to the text.
