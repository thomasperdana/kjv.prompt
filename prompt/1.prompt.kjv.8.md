1. **Install or update the skill:** copy each fenced block below verbatim to its path under `~/.claude/skills/Kjv2222/`. The nine blocks are the nine files — nothing else is needed.
2. **Run it:** in a PAI session say `kjv2222: book=Ephesians, lesson_count=5` (or any Book/Passage/Topic). The builder frames the curriculum, briefs the council, deliberates, and halts at the Blueprint checkpoint for your `APPROVE`.

````markdown
# Kjv2222

The Berean Council Reformed KJV Workbook Builder. Convenes the seven scholars of the Berean Council to construct deep, print-ready Bible study workbooks using the King James Version (KJV) exclusively. This skill combines the academic rigor, expert briefs, and verification passes of **KJV2211** with the practical 4-level question difficulty, case studies, modern examples, and advanced teacher engagement tools of **bs4-workbook**.

DEFINITION OF DONE: a leader could run the entire course from the printed workbook and Leader's Guide with zero additional preparation — every quotation exact, every participant page question-dominant with write-in lines, every answer in the Leader's Guide, the believer humbled and Christ magnified in every lesson.

## Gotchas

* **KJV Only:** Never use or quote rival translations (NIV, ESV, NASB, NKJV). Double-check all verses verbatim; if uncertain of exact wording, quote less and append `(verify)` — never approximate inside quotation marks.
* **Reformed Hermeneutics:** Let scripture interpret scripture. Focus on covenantal theology, sovereign grace, and the Law of First Mention.
* **Write-in Spaces:** Participant guides must use actual lines `__________`. Exposition and answers belong in the Leader's Guide.
* **No Placeholders:** Generate real biblical case studies and modern examples without using placeholding summaries.
* **Verify References:** Never guess a Strong's number, verse reference, or historical quotation (creed, confession, hymn, sermon). Summarize a historical voice's known position rather than fabricate its words; if uncertain, append `(verify)`.
* **Humility & Christ's Glory:** Every lesson must actively show how the believer is to humble themselves (reducing self-importance and pride) and make Christ more glorious (magnifying His name and work), delivering Humility Practices as defined above.
````

````markdown
# BuildWorkbook Workflow

This workflow executes Steps 1 through 4 of the combined Berean Council and KJV Workbook builder process.

## Voice Notification

```bash

```

Running the **BuildWorkbook** workflow in the **Kjv2222** skill to initialize combined KJV curriculum and blueprint...

## Instructions

Convene the Berean Council of seven scholars under a Reformed hermeneutic lens (Scripture interprets Scripture, covenantal theology, sovereign grace):

* **The Philologist** (Masoretic / Textus Receptus, word studies, Strong's numbers with the `(verify)` rule, archaic glosses, thee/thou vs. ye/you).
* **The Historian** (authorship, date, audience, geography, customs, politics).
* **The Literary Scholar** (genre, structure, book argument, repetitions, divides lessons).
* **The Theologian** (Reformed systematic doctrine, cross-references under the scripture-interprets-scripture rule).
* **The Church Historian** (creeds, confessions, hymns, sermons — summarize known positions, never fabricate quotations).
* **The Pastor** (sin exposed, grace offered, comfort, shepherding tender places, personal application, and Humility Practices for humbling oneself and glorifying Christ).
* **The Curriculum Architect** (learning objectives, 4-level question ladders, case studies, modern examples, write-in lines, Leader's Guide).

Execute the following steps in sequence:

### STEP 1 — Frame the Curriculum

1. Restate the user's request with every variable resolved: Book/Passage/Topic, LESSON_COUNT (default 5), AUDIENCE (default TONA small group), SESSION_LENGTH (default 75 minutes), EMPHASIS (default balanced), PACING (default checkpoint). Apply defaults silently.
2. If a TOPIC was given, choose 3-5 anchor passages and justify each in one line.
3. Divide the material into `LESSON_COUNT` lessons along the passage/book's structure.
4. State the workbook-wide Big Idea and 3-5 outcomes a participant should reach by the final lesson.

### STEP 2 — Expert Briefs

1. Print a heading for each scholar in turn.
2. Each scholar gives their 5-8 most load-bearing findings for the whole study.
3. **Minimum requirements:**
   * **Philologist:** one word-study candidate per lesson (including "Law of First Mention" candidates).
   * **Theologian:** 8-12 cross-references.
   * **Church Historian:** one historical voice per lesson.
   * **Curriculum Architect:** draft objectives per lesson.
   * **Pastor:** shepherding application and at least 3 Humility Practices for humbling oneself in the sight of the Lord and exalting Christ.

### STEP 3 — Council Deliberation

1. As Moderator, pose the three hardest questions this material raises (interpretive cruxes, apparent tensions, common misreadings).
2. Have relevant scholars discuss in dialogue.
3. Every scholar must challenge or sharpen at least one claim made in STEP 2 — naming the scholar and the claim.
4. Close each crux with a resolution from scripture, or a fair summary of the major views (routed to a View A / View B panel in the lesson where it belongs).

### STEP 4 — Workbook Blueprint

1. Print the lesson map as a markdown table with these columns: `Lesson | Title | Passage | Big Idea | Objective | Memory Verse (KJV ref)`
2. Add the front-matter and back-matter plan (incorporating teacher FAQs, facilitation tools, and student study toolkits).
3. **Pacing Rule:**
   * If `PACING = checkpoint` (default), **STOP** here and wait for the user's reply (`APPROVE` or adjustments).
   * If `PACING = run-all`, state working assumptions and proceed immediately to `Workflows/DraftLessons.md` to draft the first lesson.
````

````markdown
# DraftLessons Workflow

This workflow executes Step 5 of the combined workbook builder process, drafting the lessons. It owns THE 17-POINT LESSON TEMPLATE — other workflows (ExpandLesson, AddLesson) read it from here.

## Voice Notification

```bash
curl -s -X POST http://localhost:31337/notify \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"message": "Running the DraftLessons workflow in the Kjv2222 skill to draft lessons using the 17-point template"}' \
  > /dev/null 2>&1 &
```

Running the **DraftLessons** workflow in the **Kjv2222** skill to draft lessons using the 17-point template...

## Instructions

Draft the lessons one by one, in order, according to the approved Workbook Blueprint.

For each lesson, use this fixed, 17-point template:

1. **Heading** — Lesson number, title, passage, Big Idea, objective: one line each.
2. **Open** — A prayer focus (suggesting Psalm 119:18) and one warm-up question with write-in lines `__________`.
3. **Setting the Scene** — The Historian's context in 150-250 words.
4. **Read & Mark** — The full passage printed in the KJV with a marking key: circle repeated words, underline commands, box names of God, double-underline promises. Passages over ~25 verses appear in marked sections.
5. **Observe (Level 1: Easy)** — 5-8 "what does it say?" questions anchored to specific verses, write-in lines under each. Focus on key KJV words, who is speaking/addressed, and literal text facts.
6. **Mind the Language** — Flagged archaic KJV words; the participant writes the modern sense beside each before checking the Glossary.
7. **Word Study** — A table with columns: `KJV word | verse | original word (transliterated, Strong's #) | sense | participant lookup`. If uncertain of a Strong's number, write `(verify)` instead of guessing. The participant lookup column directs: look up another KJV occurrence (checking the Law of First Mention) and write what it adds.
8. **Search the Scriptures** — A chain of 3-6 cross-references with a summary line for each, closing with one synthesis line: what do these passages together teach? (Reformed lens: Scripture interprets Scripture).
9. **Interpret (Level 2: Medium)** — 3-5 "what does it mean?" questions. Disputed points must appear as View A / View B panels with proof texts and a "which view does the text best support, and why?" write-in.
10. **Case Study: Scripture in Action** — A biblical case study showing a Bible character (e.g. Abraham, David, Paul) applying or misapplying the core lesson truth, closing with a reflection question and write-in lines.
11. **Modern Example** — A realistic 21st-century scenario showing a believer facing a cultural or faith challenge, resolving it by applying the core text, and experiencing the biblical result. Add a reflection question with write-in lines.
12. **Voices from Church History** — One short creed, confession, hymn, or sermon excerpt with a reflection line. Quote only wording you are certain of; otherwise summarize the voice's known position and mark `(verify)`.
13. **Doctrine** — One paragraph connecting the passage to the whole counsel of God (Reformed Covenantal perspective), then "state it in your own words" lines.
14. **Apply (Level 3: Difficult)** — Head (believe), Heart (love), Hands (do) questions connecting the topic to the gospel of grace. Include questions on how to humble oneself before God and glorify Christ, closing with an "I will..." concrete commitment statement.
15. **Christocentric & Humility (Level 4)** — Explain that every problem found in the scripture, the answer is Jesus; explain that every revelation or principle found in the scripture is found in Jesus, because in Jesus is hidden all the wisdom of God (Colossians 2:3). Detail how the passage calls us to humble ourselves before God and make Christ glorious, delivering Humility Practices for daily use. Add a reflection question with write-in lines.
16. **Memory Verse** — KJV verbatim, with copy-it-out lines beneath.
17. **Gather** — 4 group discussion questions with leader timing and facilitation cues (e.g. the 7-10 seconds silence technique, comparing Big Ideas) that fit `SESSION_LENGTH`.

## Rules

* **Write-in lines:** Always use `__________` (at least 2-3 lines per question) so that the participant can write in their answers.
* **Answers:** Participant pages carry questions and activities; exposition and answers live in the Leader's Guide (Back Matter). Do not put the answers in the participant pages.
* **Conformity Check:** After drafting each lesson, perform a one-line conformity check: print `PASS` or `FIX` for each of the 17 template components. Correct any `FIX` before moving on.
* **Token Limits:** If output limits approach, stop at a lesson boundary and print: *Stopping at lesson [N] boundary. Reply CONTINUE to draft the next lesson.*
````

````markdown
```

Running the **BackMatter** workflow in the **Kjv2222** skill to assemble back matter and Reformed leader guide...

## Instructions

Assemble the following back-matter components:

1. **How to Use This Workbook** — Instructions for both individual study tracks (using the Law of First Mention, Webster's 1828) and group discussion tracks.
2. **Glossary** — List every flagged archaic KJV word from the lessons alongside its modern definition/gloss.
3. **Leader's Guide:**
   * Provide suggested answers with verse support for every single numbered participant question from the lessons (all four levels of the question ladder), highlighting the Humility Practices for daily application.
   * Design a group meeting timing plan that fits `SESSION_LENGTH` (default 75 minutes).
   * Add the Pastor's shepherding notes for questions that touch tender or sensitive places.
4. **Teacher FAQ:**
   * *Q: What if a student uses a different Bible version and gets confused by the KJV wording?*
   * *Q: How do I handle someone who totally misunderstands a topic?*
   * *Q: What if we don't get through all 4 levels of difficulty?*
5. **Advanced Teacher Hacks & Tricks:**
   * **Silent Processing:** Comfortably wait 7 to 10 seconds in silence after asking a question before answering it.
   * **Group Engagement:** Let the group read the verse aloud together, or ask which word jumps out to them most.
   * **The "Cross-Reference" Hack:** Keeping extra cross-references in reserve to re-engage the discussion.
   * **The "Application" Trick:** Facilitating the "I will..." commitments at the end of the session.
   * **The "Humbling & Exalting" Hack:** Helping participants transition from intellectual learning to humbling themselves under God's hand and exalting Christ, using the Humility Practices.
6. **Going Deeper** — Suggest further passages and study questions for the weeks beyond.

## Rules

* **Token Limits:** The Leader's Guide is the longest output in the workbook. If output limits approach, stop at a component or lesson-answer boundary and print: *Stopping at [boundary]. Reply CONTINUE to assemble the next part.*
````
