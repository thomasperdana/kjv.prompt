## WHAT v8.1 ADDS

v8.0 repaired the bundle's structure and made every definition single-source. v8.1 adds the two things Jesus says He came to bring, and makes the workbook *hand them over* — on the page, in the participant's own handwriting:

- **LIFE** — John 10:10. Every lesson names the theft (the specific death or deadness the passage exposes), shows where the life is (Christ, with the verse), shows how it passes through a believer to others, and gives the participant a **Life Planner** to fill in with a name.
- **LIGHT** — John 8:12. Every lesson names the darkness, shows where the light is, shows how it is carried, and gives the participant a **Light Planner** — starting with one dark thing in his own heart before he carries light anywhere else.

The two are one gift, joined in one verse: "In him was life; and the life was the light of men." — John 1:4 (KJV).

**The discipline that keeps this from becoming self-help:** the participant never originates life or light — he receives and reflects, "for without me ye can do nothing" (John 15:5). SOURCE DISCIPLINE (defined once in `SKILL.md`) enforces it, and V18 verifies it.

**Structural consequence:** the question ladder grows from four levels to five (Level 5 — Life & Light: Bring It), the lesson template from 17 points to 19, and the verification pass from V1-V15 to V1-V18. Every file in this bundle uses those numbers consistently — the drift class v8.0 eliminated stays eliminated.

````markdown
# Kjv2222

The Berean Council Reformed KJV Workbook Builder — v8.1, Life & Light. Convenes the seven scholars of the Berean Council to construct deep, print-ready Bible study workbooks using the King James Version (KJV) exclusively. ## Shared Definitions

- **THE 5-LEVEL QUESTION LADDER** — Level 1: Observe (Easy) · Level 2: Interpret (Medium) · Level 3: Apply (Difficult) · Level 4: Christocentric & Humility · Level 5: Life & Light (Bring It). Every lesson climbs all five levels; every workflow uses this exact ladder.
- **HUMILITY PRACTICES** — concrete tips, tricks, and daily hacks: specific mental exercises, habits, prayers, and spiritual practices for humbling oneself in the sight of the Lord and exalting Christ. Wherever this skill calls for Humility Practices, deliver at least 3, each doable within one day.
- **THE LIFE PATHWAY** (John 10:10) — how a lesson teaches the participant to bring life, in four fixed slots:
  (a) **THE THEFT** — the specific death, deadness, barrenness, or bondage this passage exposes, named in the passage's own terms, not in generalities.
  (b) **THE SOURCE** — where the life actually is: Christ Himself, with the verse that says so, and the means He uses to give it (His word, His Spirit, His cross, His resurrection).
  (c) **THE CHANNEL** — how that life passes *through* a believer to others as this passage frames it: words that quicken rather than kill ("Death and life are in the power of the tongue" — Proverbs 18:21), the gospel spoken plainly, forgiveness released, a burden carried, a hungry man fed, the barren thing tended until it bears.
  (d) **LIFE PRACTICES** — at least 3, each doable within one day, at least one INWARD (how this believer receives Christ's life today) and at least one OUTWARD (how he carries it to a specific named person today).
- **THE ABUNDANCE GUARD** — printed in every lesson: "more abundantly" in John 10:10 is fullness of life in Christ, not material prosperity, guaranteed ease, or freedom from suffering. Refuse that misreading out loud rather than leaving it unaddressed.
- **THE LIGHT PATHWAY** (John 8:12) — how a lesson teaches the participant to bring light, in four fixed slots:
  (a) **THE DARKNESS** — the specific darkness this passage addresses: ignorance, concealment, confusion, self-deceit, or the works done because no one is watching.
  (b) **THE SOURCE** — Christ the light, with the verse; and how that light reaches a man ("For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." — 2 Corinthians 4:6).
  (c) **THE LAMP** — how light is carried: Scripture opened to someone in the dark ("Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." — Psalm 119:105), truth told where a lie was convenient, hidden sin brought into the open and confessed, works of darkness reproved ("And have no fellowship with the works of darkness, but rather reprove them." — Ephesians 5:11), and good works done visibly so that God, not the doer, is glorified ("Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven." — Matthew 5:16).
  (d) **LIGHT PRACTICES** — at least 3, each doable within one day, at least one that turns the light on the participant's OWN heart first, and at least one that carries light to another.
- **THE LOVE GUARD** — printed in every lesson: light exposes, but a believer who exposes without love is glare, not light. Reproof is never license for accusation, contempt, or public shaming.
- **SOURCE DISCIPLINE** — never present the participant as the origin of life or light. He is a channel and a reflector: "for without me ye can do nothing" (John 15:5). Every Humility, Life, and Light Practice must trace back to Christ with a verse, and no practice may be written so that it works by the participant's own energy, sincerity, or technique. Humility is not a separate topic from life and light — it is the posture in which both are received.
- **THE LIFE & LIGHT PLANNERS** — the two participant write-in tables in lesson template points 16 and 17, where the participant names a person to carry life to and a dark place to carry light into, and dates it.
- **THE 19-POINT LESSON TEMPLATE** — lives in `Workflows/DraftLessons.md`. Any workflow that drafts or re-drafts a lesson reads it there first.

## Gotchas

* **KJV Only:** Never use or quote rival translations (NIV, ESV, NASB, NKJV). Double-check all verses verbatim; if uncertain of exact wording, quote less and append `(verify)` — never approximate inside quotation marks.
* **Reformed Hermeneutics:** Let scripture interpret scripture. Focus on covenantal theology, sovereign grace, and the Law of First Mention.
* **Write-in Spaces:** Participant guides must use actual lines `__________`. Exposition and answers belong in the Leader's Guide.
* **No Placeholders:** Generate real biblical case studies and modern examples without using placeholding summaries. The same rule binds the Life and Light Planners: real names go on the participant's line, not sample text.
* **Verify References:** Never guess a Strong's number, verse reference, or historical quotation (creed, confession, hymn, sermon). Summarize a historical voice's known position rather than fabricate its words; if uncertain, append `(verify)`.
* **Humility & Christ's Glory:** Every lesson must actively show how the believer is to humble themselves (reducing self-importance and pride) and make Christ more glorious (magnifying His name and work), delivering Humility Practices as defined above.
* **Life & Light:** Every lesson must complete THE LIFE PATHWAY and THE LIGHT PATHWAY, print THE ABUNDANCE GUARD and THE LOVE GUARD, and carry both Planners with real write-in lines. A lesson that informs without bringing life, or heats without bringing light, is not finished — it is a `FIX`.
* **Source Discipline:** Never write a practice, answer, or example that makes the participant the origin of life or light. Trace every practice to Christ with a verse.
````

````markdown
```

Running the **BuildWorkbook** workflow in the **Kjv2222** skill to initialize combined KJV curriculum and blueprint...

## Instructions

Convene the Berean Council of seven scholars under a Reformed hermeneutic lens (Scripture interprets Scripture, covenantal theology, sovereign grace):

* **The Philologist** (Masoretic / Textus Receptus, word studies, Strong's numbers with the `(verify)` rule, archaic glosses, thee/thou vs. ye/you). Where the material speaks of life, death, light, or darkness, states which sense the KJV carries there.
* **The Historian** (authorship, date, audience, geography, customs, politics).
* **The Literary Scholar** (genre, structure, book argument, repetitions, divides lessons). Traces the life/death and light/darkness contrasts the material itself builds.
* **The Theologian** (Reformed systematic doctrine, cross-references under the scripture-interprets-scripture rule). Establishes Christ as the single source of both life and light (John 1:4; John 10:10; John 8:12) — never the participant, never the workbook, never the method.
* **The Church Historian** (creeds, confessions, hymns, sermons — summarize known positions, never fabricate quotations).
* **The Pastor** (sin exposed, grace offered, comfort, shepherding tender places, personal application, Humility Practices, and THE LIFE PATHWAY and THE LIGHT PATHWAY for each lesson).
* **The Curriculum Architect** (learning objectives, the 5-level question ladder, case studies, modern examples, the Life & Light Planners, write-in lines, Leader's Guide).

Execute the following steps in sequence:

### STEP 1 — Frame the Curriculum

1. Restate the user's request with every variable resolved: Book/Passage/Topic, LESSON_COUNT (default 5), AUDIENCE (default TONA small group), SESSION_LENGTH (default 75 minutes), EMPHASIS (default balanced), PACING (default checkpoint). Apply defaults silently.
2. If a TOPIC was given, choose 3-5 anchor passages and justify each in one line.
3. Divide the material into `LESSON_COUNT` lessons along the passage/book's structure.
4. State the workbook-wide Big Idea and 3-5 outcomes a participant should reach by the final lesson.
5. State the workbook-wide **Life & Light Frame** in two sentences: the death this material speaks into and the life Christ gives instead; the darkness it exposes and the light Christ gives instead.

### STEP 2 — Expert Briefs

1. Print a heading for each scholar in turn.
2. Each scholar gives their 5-8 most load-bearing findings for the whole study.
3. **Minimum requirements:**
   * **Philologist:** one word-study candidate per lesson (including "Law of First Mention" candidates).
   * **Theologian:** 8-12 cross-references, plus the line from this material to Christ as the source of life and light (John 1:4).
   * **Church Historian:** one historical voice per lesson.
   * **Curriculum Architect:** draft objectives per lesson, at least one of them a *doing* objective — something the participant can do this week that brings life or light.
   * **Pastor:** shepherding application; at least 3 Humility Practices for humbling oneself in the sight of the Lord and exalting Christ; and, per lesson, THE LIFE PATHWAY slots (a)-(c) and THE LIGHT PATHWAY slots (a)-(c). The full practice sets (≥3 Life, ≥3 Light) are due in `Workflows/DraftLessons.md`.

### STEP 3 — Council Deliberation

1. As Moderator, pose the three hardest questions this material raises (interpretive cruxes, apparent tensions, common misreadings).
2. Have relevant scholars discuss in dialogue.
3. Every scholar must challenge or sharpen at least one claim made in STEP 2 — naming the scholar and the claim.
4. Close each crux with a resolution from scripture, or a fair summary of the major views (routed to a View A / View B panel in the lesson where it belongs).
5. Then pose a fourth, mandatory question — **THE LIFE & LIGHT AUDIT**:

   > "If a participant completed this whole course exactly as written, what in him would come alive, and what dark place would be lit? Name both, concretely, and say which lesson does it. If the honest answer is 'nothing but his notes,' the council names the fix before we blueprint."

   The Pastor answers first; the Theologian checks the answer against SOURCE DISCIPLINE (has anything here been made to depend on the participant's own power?); the Curriculum Architect states which lesson component carries it and whether a Level 1 participant could do it. Print the fix if one was needed.

### STEP 4 — Workbook Blueprint

1. Print the lesson map as a markdown table with these columns: `Lesson | Title | Passage | Big Idea | Objective | Memory Verse (KJV ref) | Life → Light (what comes alive · what gets lit)`
2. Add the front-matter and back-matter plan (incorporating teacher FAQs, facilitation tools, student study toolkits, and the course-long Life & Light Ledger).
3. **Pacing Rule:**
   * If `PACING = checkpoint` (default), **STOP** here and wait for the user's reply (`APPROVE` or adjustments).
   * If `PACING = run-all`, state working assumptions and proceed immediately to `Workflows/DraftLessons.md` to draft the first lesson.
````

````markdown
## Instructions

Draft the lessons one by one, in order, according to the approved Workbook Blueprint.

For each lesson, use this fixed, 19-point template:

1. **Heading** — Lesson number, title, passage, Big Idea, objective: one line each.
2. **Open** — A prayer focus (suggesting Psalm 119:18) and one warm-up question with write-in lines `__________`.
3. **Setting the Scene** — The Historian's context in 150-250 words.
4. **Read & Mark** — The full passage printed in the KJV with a marking key: circle repeated words, underline commands, box names of God, double-underline promises. Passages over ~25 verses appear in marked sections.
5. **Observe (Level 1: Easy)** — 5-8 "what does it say?" questions anchored to specific verses, write-in lines under each. Focus on key KJV words, who is speaking/addressed, and literal text facts.
6. **Mind the Language** — Flagged archaic KJV words; the participant writes the modern sense beside each before checking the Glossary.
7. **Word Study** — A table with columns: `KJV word | verse | original word (transliterated, Strong's #) | sense | participant lookup`. If uncertain of a Strong's number, write `(verify)` instead of guessing. The participant lookup column directs: look up another KJV occurrence (checking the Law of First Mention) and write what it adds.
8. **Search the Scriptures** — A chain of 3-6 cross-references with a summary line for each, closing with one synthesis line: what do these passages together teach? (Reformed lens: Scripture interprets Scripture).
9. **Interpret (Level 2: Medium)** — 3-5 "what does it mean?" questions. Disputed points must appear as View A / View B panels with proof texts and a "which view does the text best support, and why?" write-in.
10. **Case Study: Scripture in Action** — A biblical case study showing a Bible character (e.g. Abraham, David, Paul) applying or misapplying the core lesson truth, closing with a reflection question and write-in lines.
11. **Modern Example** — A realistic 21st-century scenario showing a believer facing a cultural or faith challenge, resolving it by applying the core text, and experiencing the biblical result. Add a reflection question with write-in lines.
12. **Voices from Church History** — One short creed, confession, hymn, or sermon excerpt with a reflection line. Quote only wording you are certain of; otherwise summarize the voice's known position and mark `(verify)`.
13. **Doctrine** — One paragraph connecting the passage to the whole counsel of God (Reformed Covenantal perspective), then "state it in your own words" lines.
14. **Apply (Level 3: Difficult)** — Head (believe), Heart (love), Hands (do) questions connecting the topic to the gospel of grace. Include questions on how to humble oneself before God and glorify Christ, closing with an "I will..." concrete commitment statement.
15. **Christocentric & Humility (Level 4)** — Explain that every problem found in the scripture, the answer is Jesus; explain that every revelation or principle found in the scripture is found in Jesus, because in Jesus is hidden all the wisdom of God (Colossians 2:3). Detail how the passage calls us to humble ourselves before God and make Christ glorious, delivering Humility Practices for daily use. Add a reflection question with write-in lines.
16. **Bring Life (Level 5a — John 10:10)** — Build THE LIFE PATHWAY for this passage on the participant page, in this order:
    * **The Theft** — one or two sentences naming the specific death, deadness, barrenness, or bondage this passage exposes, in the passage's own terms.
    * **The Source** — the Christ-and-verse line: where the life is, and how He gives it.
    * **The Channel** — 2-3 sentences on how that life passes through a believer to another person as this passage frames it.
    * **Questions** — 3-4 Level 5 questions with write-in lines: what has the thief stolen where I live? what does Christ give instead, and in whose life am I withholding it?
    * **THE LIFE PLANNER** — a write-in table:

      | Who around me is dead, dying, or deadened? (name) | What has the thief stolen there? | What does Christ give instead (verse) | What I will do about it this week | Done? |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `____` |

    * **Life Practices** — at least 3 from THE LIFE PATHWAY (d), each doable within one day, at least one inward and one outward, each traced to Christ with a verse per SOURCE DISCIPLINE.
    * **Commitment** — `I will bring life to __________ by __________ before __________ (day).`
    * **THE ABUNDANCE GUARD**, printed in italics on the page: *abundant life is fullness of life in Christ — not money, not ease, not freedom from suffering.*
17. **Bring Light (Level 5b — John 8:12)** — Build THE LIGHT PATHWAY for this passage on the participant page, in this order:
    * **The Darkness** — one or two sentences naming the specific darkness this passage addresses.
    * **The Source** — the Christ-and-verse line: where the light is, and how it reaches a man.
    * **The Lamp** — 2-3 sentences on how the light is carried, in this passage's terms.
    * **Questions** — 3-4 Level 5 questions with write-in lines, the first one turned inward: what am I keeping in the dark, and what would change if I brought it out?
    * **THE LIGHT PLANNER** — a write-in table, the first row reserved for the participant's own heart:

      | Where is it dark? (mine first, then around me) | What is hidden there? | What light does Christ shine (verse) | How I carry it there this week — in love, not accusation | Done? |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `____` |

    * **Light Practices** — at least 3 from THE LIGHT PATHWAY (d), each doable within one day, at least one self-examining and one outward, each traced to Christ with a verse per SOURCE DISCIPLINE.
    * **Commitment** — `I will carry light into __________ by __________ before __________ (day).`
    * **THE LOVE GUARD**, printed in italics on the page: *light exposes, but light without love is glare; reproof is never license for contempt or public shaming.*
18. **Memory Verse** — KJV verbatim, with copy-it-out lines beneath.
19. **Gather** — 4 group discussion questions with leader timing and facilitation cues (e.g. the 7-10 seconds silence technique, comparing Big Ideas) that fit `SESSION_LENGTH`. One of the four must be the naming round: each member reads out the name on his Life Planner and the dark place on his Light Planner.

## Rules

* **Write-in lines:** Always use `__________` (at least 2-3 lines per question) so that the participant can write in their answers.
* **Answers:** Participant pages carry questions, planners, practices, and activities; exposition and answers live in the Leader's Guide (Back Matter). Do not put the answers in the participant pages.
* **Life & Light Rule:** No lesson ships without both Planners present with real write-in lines, both commitment lines, both guards printed, and ≥3 Life Practices and ≥3 Light Practices. Practices must never be written so that they work by the participant's own energy — trace each to Christ with a verse (SOURCE DISCIPLINE).
* **Conformity Check:** After drafting each lesson, perform a one-line conformity check: print `PASS` or `FIX` for each of the 19 template components. Correct any `FIX` before moving on.
* **Token Limits:** If output limits approach, stop at a lesson boundary and print: *Stopping at lesson [N] boundary. Reply CONTINUE to draft the next lesson.*
````

````markdown
   | Lesson | The name I carried life to | What I did | The dark place I carried light into | What I did | Answered prayer / result |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` | `__________` |

   Add a closing line beneath it: this page records what Christ did through you, not what you achieved — "for without me ye can do nothing" (John 15:5).
5. **Teacher FAQ:**
   * *Q: What if a student uses a different Bible version and gets confused by the KJV wording?*
   * *Q: How do I handle someone who totally misunderstands a topic?*
   * *Q: What if we don't get through all 5 levels of difficulty?*
   * *Q: What if someone reads "life more abundantly" as money, health, or an easy life?* (Answer from THE ABUNDANCE GUARD.)
   * *Q: What if someone uses "being light" as permission to expose other people?* (Answer from THE LOVE GUARD.)

6. **Advanced Teacher Hacks & Tricks:**
   * **Silent Processing:** Comfortably wait 7 to 10 seconds in silence after asking a question before answering it.
   * **Group Engagement:** Let the group read the verse aloud together, or ask which word jumps out to them most.
   * **The "Cross-Reference" Hack:** Keeping extra cross-references in reserve to re-engage the discussion.
   * **The "Application" Trick:** Facilitating the "I will..." commitments at the end of the session.
   * **The "Humbling & Exalting" Hack:** Helping participants transition from intellectual learning to humbling themselves under God's hand and exalting Christ, using the Humility Practices.
   * **The "Bring Life" Hack:** Run the naming round out loud — a name spoken in the group is acted on far more often than a name only written. Then open the next session by asking who did it, gently and without shaming the ones who did not.
   * **The "Bring Light" Hack:** The leader goes first on the Light Planner's inward row, briefly and appropriately. A group will only bring its own darkness into the light behind someone who did it first.
7. **Going Deeper** — Suggest further passages and study questions for the weeks beyond, including one passage each on life and on light.
````
